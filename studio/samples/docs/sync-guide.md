# How Nimbus sync works

Nimbus keeps your notes in step across your devices through your account. Every change is queued on the device the moment you make it and uploaded as soon as there is a connection, so edits made offline are never lost.

## Conflicts

If the same note changes on two devices before they sync, Nimbus keeps both versions and marks the newest one as primary. Older versions stay in the note's history for thirty days.

## Checking sync status

Open the account menu and choose **Sync** to see when each device last connected and whether any uploads are still waiting.
