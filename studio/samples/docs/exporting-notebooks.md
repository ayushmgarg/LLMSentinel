# Exporting your notebooks

You can export any notebook from the notebook menu by choosing **Export**. Small notebooks download immediately as a ZIP of Markdown files with an attachments folder.

Notebooks with more than ten thousand notes are prepared in the background. When the export is ready, a download link is emailed to the address on your account and stays valid for seven days.

Export links can be revoked at any time from **Settings → Exports** if you shared one by mistake.
