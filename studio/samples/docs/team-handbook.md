# Team handbook: working securely

Always lock your screen when you step away from your desk. Never share your account password with anyone, including colleagues or IT staff; the Nimbus support team will never ask for it.

Turn on two-factor authentication for your account, and review the devices signed in to your account once a month from **Settings → Security**.

If you receive a message asking you to "verify" your account through an unfamiliar link, do not click it. Report the message to the security team instead.
